# Snake Queue

A to-do list where every task is a piece of a snake, on an old LCD screen. The more you have to do, the longer it gets; the piece touching the head is next.

Live at [jotaverse.com.br/snake-queue](https://jotaverse.com.br/snake-queue/).

- **add**: type and press Enter (`/` focuses the field)
- **read**: hover or focus a piece to see its task under the board; with a piece focused, the arrow keys move along the queue. On a phone, tap a piece
- **finish**: click a piece or press Enter on it; `Del` finishes the first (or the focused one). On a phone, tap the same piece again
- **edit**: double-click, or `E` on a focused piece. On a phone, press and hold a piece
- **undo**: `Ctrl+Z`, or *undo* on the message that pops up
- **screen**: four LCD colors; the page follows your light/dark setting unless you pick one
- **handheld**: optional, off by default and only on wide screens; turn it on under *screen* and the LCD is laid onto a rendered flip phone (`assets/flip.webp`, geometry in `assets/flip.json`)
